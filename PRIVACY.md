# Versed Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 30, 2026*

Versed collects no data. Here is what that means in plain language.

## What Versed collects
Nothing. Versed doesn't collect, send, sell, or share any information about you.

## What stays on your phone
Versed remembers a few things so it works the way you expect. They're stored only on your iPhone and never leave it:
- Verses you save
- Where you left off reading
- Which days you read, for your reading streak (only the date, not what you read)
- Your settings: light or dark mode, and the daily reminder's on/off choice and time
- Whether you've seen the first-time intro

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## No accounts, ads, or tracking
- No sign-up or account
- No ads
- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No connection to any server. Versed works without the internet.

## Daily reminder
The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, your iPhone schedules it by itself. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Versed's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings app.

## Links to other sites
The "Open This Week's Lesson" button opens a page on churchofjesuschrist.org, in the Gospel Library app if you have it, or in Safari. Those are run by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, not by Versed, and their own privacy policies apply. Versed doesn't share anything with them.

## Children
Versed collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
