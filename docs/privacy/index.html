Versed Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Versed collects no data. It doesn't collect, send, sell, or share any information about you.

What stays on your phone

Versed remembers a few things so it works the way you expect. They're kept only on your iPhone and never leave it:

  • Verses you save
  • Where you left off reading
  • The days you read, for your reading streak
  • Your settings, like light or dark mode and the daily reminder

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

Versed has no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics. It doesn't connect to any server. The optional daily reminder is scheduled by your iPhone itself.

Links to other sites

The "Open This Week's Lesson" button opens a page on churchofjesuschrist.org, in Gospel Library or Safari. That site has its own privacy policy. Versed doesn't share anything with it.
