How to use Versed

  • Start a plan: on Today, tap Add a Plan. Pick how long to take, and whether to read by day or by week.
  • Read: tap a plan on Today, then swipe up for the next verse. You can follow more than one plan.
  • Change or remove a plan: open it on Today and tap Details.
  • Pick another week: use the menu at the top of a weekly reading, or the Come, Follow Me cover in the Library.
  • Save a verse: double-tap it, or tap the heart. Find it later in the Saved tab.
  • Read any chapter: open the Library and pick a book. Tap + to add or remove books.
  • Settings: open the Settings tab for appearance, the daily reminder, and the intro.

Common questions

Where are my saved verses kept?
Only on your iPhone. They aren't backed up or synced, so deleting the app deletes them.

Does Versed need the internet?
No. Only the lesson link at the end of each week opens a web page.

My daily reminder isn't showing up.
Make sure it's on in Versed's Settings, then check your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > Versed.

Where does the scripture text come from?
A public-domain edition from bcbooks/scriptures-json.
